# Toledo Cathedral: sources, route and doubts

Set: c00 opening, c01 nave and Mozarabic chapel, c02 treasury, c03 choir, c04 main chapel, c05 chapter house, c06 Transparente and red hats, c07 Santiago chapel, c08 sacristy and Portocarrero, c09 cloister and tower, c10 closing. About 5,490 spoken words. Personal name used twice (c00, c10). Replaces toledo_sights/scripts/s05-cathedral.txt as the detailed tour (s05 left untouched).

## Route established
- Entrance for the cultural visit is always the Puerta Llana, Calle Cardenal Cisneros 1, south side; the only street-level door, neoclassical, 1800. Tickets in the cathedral shop opposite. https://www.catedralprimada.es/en/cultural-visit/tickets-toledo-cathedral/ ; es.wikipedia Catedral de Santa María de Toledo.
- Basic ticket covers Main Chapel, Transparente, Choir, Sacristy and gallery, Chapter Hall, Cloister, principal chapels (official tickets page). Treasury listed by TCLM.
- Tower: official tower page (https://www.catedralprimada.es/en/?p=3274) does not say whether the climb is included or timed. Third-party sources disagree (one 2026 blog: included "if available"; Lonely Planet: small add-on with upper cloister). Script says "may need its own ticket or a time slot; ask at the shop".
- No official step-by-step order found. Order used follows the building's geography (south entrance, west end, northwest treasury, centre choir, main chapel, south ambulatory chapter house, Transparente, east chapels, north side sacristy, north cloister), cross-checked with https://www.thegeographicalcure.com/post/guide-to-toledo-cathedral (treasury, then cloister door to its left; chapter house behind main chapel on the right; sacristy last). Directions say "check the floor plan" wherever uncertain.
- Hours and prices deliberately not stated.

## Isabella "horrified" anecdote
- Not found in the cathedral. The "esta nonada" remark belongs to San Juan de los Reyes and is already told in toledo_sights s02 (sourced there to the Prado encyclopedia). Hispanopedia also says she was disappointed with Guas's first result there (unsourced). Not reused here.
- Cathedral Isabella stories found and used in c04: she and the archbishop overrode the chapter so Mendoza's tomb could go in the main chapel, with a wall demolished (Colomina Torner, Real Academia de Toledo, via search; Carrero Santamaría: chapter granted the north bay "no sin discusión"). Her quip "the cardinal's three beautiful sins" about his sons (en.wikipedia Toledo Cathedral). The "knocked through at night" detail was NOT found; omitted.
- Possibly the user is conflating with s05's Transparente line "some were horrified".

## Per track
- c00: Visigothic church (587 consecration tradition), main mosque, 1085 surrender terms, Constance and Bernard of Sedirac seize mosque while king away, Mariana reports king's fury and order to execute; alfaquí Abu Walid legend, effigy on the "pilar del alfaquí", honour undocumented (es.wikipedia). First stone 1226, closed 1493; one tower, the other base under the Mozarabic dome (es/en wikipedia).
- c01: Saint Christopher mural, Gabriel de Rueda 1638, between San Eugenio chapel and Lions' Door (es/en wikipedia). Chapel of the Descent, second pillar from the west, on site of Visigothic high altar (official chapel page); stone of the Virgin's feet behind a grille (eldebate 2026; Real Academia, via search). Mozarabic chapel, Cisneros c.1500 to 1510, printed rite 1500, daily Mass, Oran murals by Borgoña (official page). Duel and fire legend (liturgicalartsjournal; medievalists.net; versions disagree on which book). 
- c02: Arfe 1517 to 1524 for Cisneros, about 260 statuettes, Corpus procession (en.wikipedia); Isabella's inner monstrance by Jaume Aimerich, bought by the canons about 1505 (es.wikipedia). Columbus gold: tradition, stated as fact on es.wikipedia without source. Bible of Saint Louis, three volumes, how it came to Toledo disputed (es.wikipedia conflicts internally).
- c03: Rodrigo Alemán 1489 to 1495, fifty-four seats/panels of the Granada war, most identifiable (official choir page https://www.catedralprimada.es/en/?p=3258 ; dialnet article citing Carriazo 1927). Upper stalls Bigarny and Berruguete, Berruguete's Transfiguration over the throne (official). Virgen Blanca 14th century (album-online; Matthiesen catalogue).
- c04: retablo 1498 to 1504, Calvary at top, Villalpando grille with Charles V arms, royal tombs moved (official main chapel page). Mendoza tomb, first Castilian Renaissance tomb, attribution Fancelli or Sansovino (official; wikipedia). Shepherd Martín Alhaja on a pillar of the high altar screen (en.wikipedia).
- c05: ceiling 1508 to 1510 by Diego López and Francisco de Lara; Borgoña portraits from 1508, frescoes c.1511 (es.wikipedia).
- c06: Tomé and four sons, 1729 to 1732, Archbishop Diego de Astorga buried at its foot, 200,000 ducats, poem "eighth wonder of the world" (en.wikipedia El Transparente; official page). 
- c07: Chapel built for Álvaro de Luna, Hanequin de Bruselas; Juana Pimentel "la Triste Condesa"; altarpiece commissioned by María de Luna 1488 (official Santiago page; hispanopedia). Gilded bronze tomb statue destroyed c.1441 after Infante Enrique entered Toledo, and his joke (Spanish sources via search, citing Hernán Núñez/Mena). Execution Valladolid June 1453, head shown nine days, basin for alms (El Debate 2025; old account; Prado painting "Pidiendo limosna para enterrar a Don Álvaro de Luna").
- c08: Expolio 1577 to 1579, via Luis de Castilla, objections to three Marys and heads above Christ, 950 vs 350 ducats, figures never removed (en.wikipedia El Espolio; Sancho San Román 1962; EFE note). Apostolado, Goya, Van Dyck; Titian Paul III and Caravaggio John the Baptist in the vestry (en.wikipedia; official sacristy page names Caravaggio, Titian). Giordano vault: Virgin imposing the chasuble on Ildefonsus (newliturgicalmovement; Kenyon record). Portocarrero epitaph "Hic iacet pulvis, cinis et nihil", tomb in Capilla del Sagrario (es.wikipedia).
- c09: cloister begun 14 August 1389 on the alcaná, finished 1425, fire legend about Tenorio (es.wikipedia). Bayeu and Maella frescoes (en.wikipedia; El Debate 2026). Tower 300 steps; Campana Gorda cast 1753 by Gargallo (official; es.wikipedia says 1755, script says "seventeen fifties"), over 17 tonnes, about 3.5 m, cracked at first ringing; clapper blamed (es.wikipedia).
