# title: Stop 0. The Cathedral: A broken promise
# stop: 0
# room: Outside, by the Puerta Llana on Calle Cardenal Cisneros

## The king is away

N: Welcome, Monica. Play this outside, before you go in, or while you wait in the queue at the Puerta Llana, the plain door on the side street. It is the only door of this cathedral with no steps up to it.

...

N: Picture this spot nine hundred and forty years ago. No Gothic towers. A great mosque, the main mosque of a Muslim city. And before the mosque, people said, a Visigothic church. The ground under your feet has changed faith twice.

N: Then a Christian king, Alfonso the Sixth of Castile, takes Toledo. The city surrenders on terms. One of the terms is a promise. The Muslims of Toledo will keep their great mosque.

N: It lasted about a year.

## The queen and the monk

N: The king rides out of the city. Behind him stay two people who never liked that promise. His French queen, Constance. And the new archbishop, Bernard, a monk from the great abbey of Cluny in Burgundy.

N: While the king is away, they send armed men into the mosque. They set up an altar. They hang a bell in the minaret. The mosque is a church.

N: The king hears, and he is furious. One later chronicler says he swore to put the guilty to death.

A: Now the legend. The Muslims of Toledo, it is said, sent their wisest man, a jurist named Abu Walid, to meet the angry king. And the jurist begged him to forgive. Let it stand, he said. Do not shed blood for our mosque. The king's anger cooled. And the cathedral never forgot. Inside, on a pillar of the main chapel, they say a carved figure honours that Muslim peacemaker.

N: That is legend; no document proves it. You will meet him later.

## Two and a half centuries of stone

N: The old mosque served as cathedral for well over a hundred years. Then, in twelve twenty-six, a king laid the first stone of this one. It took over two and a half centuries. If you walked past the main front, in the square, you saw it. Two towers were planned. Only one was ever finished. The other stops short and wears a small dome, like a hat.

...

N: The archbishops of Toledo were the richest churchmen in Spain. Almost everything inside was paid for by such men, and every one wanted to be remembered.

## How to use this tour

N: Nine stops follow, in the order you will walk, with a short goodbye at the end. Each stop tells you where to go. When you hear a pause, stop the track, look, then play on. Things get moved. If something is not where I say, check the floor plan.

N: The bell tower climb may need its own ticket or a time slot. Ask at the ticket shop before you go in.

N: If you only have thirty minutes, do these five. The war carved in wood around the choir. The hole in the roof behind the high altar. The painted ceiling of the chapter house. El Greco's red robe in the sacristy. And the tower of silver in the treasury.

...

N: Now go in through the Puerta Llana. Once inside, look along the walls near the door for a painted giant. When you find him, play stop one.
