# Toledo sights: sources and doubts

Active set: s01 Santo Tomé, s02 San Juan de los Reyes, s03 Cristo de la Luz, s04 San Román, s05 Cathedral, s06 closing. Retired (skipped by her, kept with leading underscore): _s01-el-transito, _s03-santa-maria-la-blanca.

## s01 Santo Tomé, Burial of the Count of Orgaz
- Lord of Orgaz d. 1323; legend of Saints Stephen and Augustine; Núñez commission, contract 18 Mar 1586, appraisal clause; El Greco accepted the lower first estimate (13,200 reales, figure not used); Núñez reading at right; Philip II in heaven while alive; Jorge Manuel with handkerchief signed and dated 1578, his birth year; self-portrait above the raised hand of a Santiago knight. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Burial_of_the_Count_of_Orgaz ; https://www.britannica.com/topic/Burial-of-the-Conde-de-Orgaz
- UNVERIFIED this session (from memory): the Orgaz townspeople stopping the annual bequest and Núñez winning a lawsuit against them before the commission. Widely told; recheck.
- "Separate entrance/room": phrased as "check at the door".
- No value figure used.

## s02 San Juan de los Reyes
- Founded after Toro 1476; first intended as royal burial place; Isabella switched to Granada; interim burial in the San Francisco convent in the Alhambra (now the Parador), moved to the Royal Chapel 1521. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Chapel_of_Granada ; https://capillarealgranada.com/en/the-founding-monarchs/
- Chains of freed Christian captives; sources disagree on which prisons (Granada, Málaga/Almería/Baeza, Ronda), so script gives the disagreement in the A voice. https://worldcitytrail.com/2025/02/28/monasterio-de-san-juan-in-toledo/ ; https://www.lonelyplanet.com/spain/toledo/attractions/monasterio-san-juan-de-los-reyes/a/poi-sig/483194/360759
- UNVERIFIED this session (general knowledge): yoke/arrows emblems as Y/F initials; Napoleonic troops quartered and fire; eagles of Saint John in the crossing.

## s03 Cristo de la Luz
- Inscription: Ahmad ibn Hadidi patron, completed 999 (390 AH), builders Musa ibn Ali and Saada; nine differently ribbed vaults on four columns; later Christian apse with wall paintings. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosque_of_Cristo_de_la_Luz
- Roman street: granite slabs about 5 m wide with a drain beneath, found in 2006 excavations; sources disagree on dating (1st vs 2nd c.) and on exact spot (front vs back garden), so script says "garden next to the building" and "check at the door". https://es.hispanopedia.com/wiki/Cristo_de_la_Luz ; https://lamaletainquieta.com/mezquita-cristo-de-la-luz/ ; https://www.ancient-history-sites.com/sites/via-monumental-romana-toledo
- Legend (horse) referenced in one line only.

## s04 San Román
- Mudéjar 13th-c. church, museum of the Councils and Visigothic culture; horseshoe arches on reused Visigothic and Roman columns; Romanesque paintings incl. winged evangelists and archbishops. https://www.spain.info/es/lugares-interes/museo-concilios-cultura-visigoda/ ; https://cultura.castillalamancha.es/sites/default/files/2024-02/MUSEO_DE_LOS_CONCILIOS_Y_LA_CULTURA_VISIGODA.pdf
- Reconsecrated and painted by the archbishop after Las Navas de Tolosa (1212); never a mosque; Arabic inscriptions in a Christian programme. https://www.medievalists.net/2014/03/legible-text-magical-pattern-arabic-inscriptions-muslim-christian-spain/ ; Latin epigraphy: https://www.realacademiatoledo.es/downloads/publicaciones_cont1/1420/notas-de-epigrafia-medieval-toledana-con-especial-referencia-a-tres-inscripciones-conservadas-en-la-iglesia-de-san-roman.pdf
- UNVERIFIED: whether a Guarrazar replica is on display (script says "check at the door"); archbishop Jiménez de Rada fighting at Las Navas (general knowledge); originals in Madrid (MAN) and Paris (Cluny) from memory.

## s05 Cathedral
- Transparente: Narciso Tomé, c. 1729-32, hole cut in vault. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Transparente
- Disrobing: 1577-79, sacristy, price dispute to appraisal (350 ducats, not used). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Disrobing_of_Christ ; https://www.inspain.org/es/info/noticias/recibosfirmadoselgreco20141215.html . Objection to the three Marys is from weaker sources; script says "as the story goes".
- Choir: Rodrigo Alemán, lower stalls, 54 reliefs of the Granada war, c. 1489-95. https://toledocathedral.blogspot.com/2025/05/2-choir.html
- Custodia: Enrique de Arfe, commissioned by Cisneros 1515; Isabella's gold monstrance inside; Columbus gold is tradition (A voice). https://liturgicalartsjournal.com/2018/03/the-arfe-custodia-masterpiece-in-silver.html ; https://www.catedralprimada.es/en/?p=3260
- Chapter House: Cisneros, Mudéjar-Plateresque ceiling, Juan de Borgoña murals c. 1509-11, archbishop portrait series. https://www.artribune.com/?p=598680 ; https://digital.lib.buffalo.edu/items/show/25751 . Count and painters of portraits disputed; not stated. "Life of the Virgin" scene list from memory.
- UNVERIFIED: Mozarabic trial-by-fire legend (told as legend); Cisneros founding the Mozarabic chapel and the rite still celebrated there (general knowledge; frequency not stated).

## Rework round 2 (s01 to s04, deeper tracks)

### s01 Santo Tomé
- CONFIRMED (softened): bequest payments ceased after about two centuries; parish (Núñez) sued, 1569, and won back payments. https://mydailyartdisplay.uk/2011/03/13/ ; Britannica says the painting celebrates a lawsuit. Script says "from Orgaz ... payments stopped", not "villagers refused".
- Contract: materials at painter's cost, price by appraisal, part-payment by settling his debts. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Burial_of_the_Count_of_Orgaz ; https://www.artic.edu/highlights/14/support-us ; lawsuit file: https://www.toledo.es/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/cuadernos-de-archivo-secreto-1.pdf
- El Greco in Toledo: Villena palace rooms (three apartments, twenty-four rooms), musicians at dinner, lawsuits over payment, Jerónima de las Cuevas probably never married, authorised son to make will 31 Mar 1614. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Greco
- Inquisition: El Greco was INTERPRETER in the 1582 trial of the Greek Miguel Rizo (not in trouble himself; no painting-related Inquisition case found). https://www.realacademiatoledo.es/downloads/publicaciones_cont1/1563/el-proceso-inquisitorial-de-miguel-rizo-en-toledo-en-1582-y-la-intervencion-del-greco.pdf
- From memory, not re-checked: stoning of Saint Stephen painted on his dalmatic hem.

### s02 San Juan de los Reyes
- "Nonada": reported by the Prado encyclopedia entry (Capilla mayor de San Juan de los Reyes, Juan Guas; 403 to fetch, quoted via search) and Spanish guides; wording varies ("Aquesta nonada me habéis hecho?"), and some say the rejected first design was by another master. Told as a story in the A voice. https://www.museodelprado.es/aprende/enciclopedia/voz/capilla-mayor-de-san-juan-de-los-reyes-juan-guas/06f58192-1a78-48b4-89de-743b95ecd469 ; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monasterio_de_San_Juan_de_los_Reyes
- CORRECTED: yoke was Ferdinand's emblem (with Tanto monta), arrows Isabella's. The Y/F initial explanation is given as "the popular story". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoke_and_arrows ; https://www.ucm.es/bdiconografiamedieval/yugoyflechas
- CONFIRMED: 1808 Napoleonic occupation and fire; second cloister destroyed; restoration from 1881 by Arturo Mélida, who rebuilt a cloister wing and its neo-Gothic cresting. https://revistas.ucm.es/index.php/ANHA/article/view/39094 ; https://www.marcialpons.es/libros/san-juan-de-los-reyes-de-toledo/9788416242085/
- NOT VERIFIED: specific modern gargoyles. Script only says some carving is restorers' work.
- Toro: Afonso V of Portugal backing (and married to) Juana; fighting into the night; mixed result claimed by both. General knowledge, not re-checked this round.

### s03 Cristo de la Luz
- Reused Visigothic capitals on three columns; fourth remade in the 1909 restoration; facade with three openings and interlaced blind arches. https://es.hispanopedia.com/wiki/Cristo_de_la_Luz ; https://www.album-online.com/detail/en/NmQxNDdlMA/capiteles-visigodos-iglesia-cristo-luz-mezquita-bab-mardum-construida-alb7364666
- Given to the Knights of Saint John in 1186 (Alfonso VIII; one source says Alfonso VI); apse about 1187; Maiestas Domini in the apse vault, badly deteriorated. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosque_of_Cristo_de_la_Luz
- Roman road: location softened (front vs back garden disputed). Inscription paraphrase "hoping for reward from God" from memory of the standard translation.

### s04 San Román
- Painting programme: two zones separated by inscriptions; winged evangelists, archbishops, Saints Stephen and Lawrence, Last Judgement. https://cultura.castillalamancha.es/sites/default/files/2024-02/MUSEO_DE_LOS_CONCILIOS_Y_LA_CULTURA_VISIGODA.pdf
- CONFIRMED: reproductions of the Guarrazar crowns in the museum (Iberdrola restoration booklet). https://www.fundacioniberdrolaespana.org/wp-content/uploads/iglesia-san-roman-toledo-cuadernos-restauracion-recuperacion-publicaciones-fundacion-iberdrola-espana.pdf . Originals Madrid (MAN) and Paris (Cluny).
- Child king shown from the San Román tower, 1166 (Alfonso VIII): Fidel Fita lecture via core.ac.uk (OCR-garbled), told as legend. https://core.ac.uk/download/71511902.pdf
- NOT VERIFIED this round (memory): Guarrazar find in 1858 after rain by a farming couple; Suinthila crown stolen from the Royal Armoury in 1921; Third Council conversion of Reccared; dead-rising figures in the Last Judgement (script says "see if you can find them").
