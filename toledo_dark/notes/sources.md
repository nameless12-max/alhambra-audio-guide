## d01 Elvira del Campo
- Transcript (Lea's English translation, AHN Inquisición de Toledo leg. 138), torture with cords, words "tell me what you want, for I don't know what to say", no confession first session, confession four days later before torture resumed: Hassner, "The Cost of Torture", Security Studies 2020, pp. 12-13, citing Lea, History of the Inquisition of Spain vol. 3 pp. 24-26. https://gwern.net/doc/history/2020-hassner.pdf
- Arrested July 1567, pregnant, wife of notary Alonso de Moya of Madridejos, accusers two men living in her house, pork and Saturday linen, challenged twelve witnesses with two struck (incl. one with a grudge), thirteen defence witnesses, illness excuse, child born in prison, ratification next day, sentence 13 June 1568 reconciliation + confiscation + sanbenito + three years prison, commuted after about six months, left destitute/begging: https://blogs.ua.es/latortura/2010/12/29/caso-de-elvira-del-campo/ (no citations; consistent with Lea).
- Second arrest and execution: Salomon, "Spanish Marranism Re-examined", Sefarad 2007 (search snippet only: "two executed: ... Elvira del Campo (aged 66)", Cuenca auto 1592; second proceso thought lost). https://sefarad.revistas.csic.es/index.php/sefarad/article/download/449/547/1055 Script gives this in A voice, hedged.

## d02 Zocodover
- Toledo auto of Feb 1560 for Philip II and Elisabeth of Valois (b. 1545, so fourteen). https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto_de_fe ; Zocodover as site of the great autos and Brasero de la Vega outside the walls: https://www.leyendasdetoledo.com/inquisicion-toledana/ ; https://lugaresconhistoria.com/las-casas-de-la-inquisicion-toledo
- Auto as ceremony of sentences; relaxation to secular arm; strangling of those who repented before burning; sanbenitos hung in parish churches and renewed; Contreras-Henningsen figures (about 44,000 cases 1540-1700, under two per cent executed in person); total executions a few thousand, worst before about 1530: Kamen, The Spanish Inquisition: A Historical Revision (general knowledge of the book, not re-read this session).

## d03 Instruments
- Only garrucha, toca, potro; rules (end of trial, physician, notary, no blood, once only but "suspension", ratification outside chamber); Toledo 1575-1610: 1,046 trials, 123 tortured; secular courts harsher: Hassner pp. 7-10.
- Iron maiden: Siebenkees guidebook c. 1793, showmen's fakes, Nuremberg piece destroyed 1944. https://www.medievalists.net/2016/03/why-medieval-torture-devices-are-not-medieval/ ; https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/fabricating-history-the-case-of-the-iron-maiden
- Pear: Chris Bishop, nothing before nineteenth century. https://www.medievalists.net/2023/12/medieval-torture-devices/
- Only three methods documented for the Spanish Inquisition; museums' fake pieces; Robert Held's catalogue: https://theobjective.com/opinion/2024-07-06/gran-farsa-museos-inquisicion/ (403, via search summary).
- Garrote official from 1832 (Ferdinand VII); last garrotings 2 March 1974, Puig Antich in Barcelona, Heinz Chez in Tarragona. https://www.executedtoday.com/2010/03/02/1974-salvador-puig-antich-heinz-ches-last-garrote-spain/
- Poe, "The Pit and the Pendulum" (1842) set in Toledo's Inquisition prisons: known text.
