## Facts used (N voice)
- Nobleman: sitter unidentified; entered royal collection via widow of the Duke of Arco (Prado). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nobleman_with_his_Hand_on_his_Chest
- El Greco: Crete c.1541, Venice, Rome, Spain 1577; Martyrdom of St Maurice commissioned 1580, delivered 1582, rejected for altar; Espolio price dispute; Illescas hospital lawsuit; died Toledo 1614; remains moved, now lost; Adoration made for his burial chapel at Santo Domingo el Antiguo, stayed there until 20th c (Prado bought 1954). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adoration_of_the_Shepherds_(El_Greco,_Madrid)
- Mühlberg 24 April 1547; Charles had gout, carried in a litter; Elector John Frederick captured; Titian painted at Augsburg 1548; armour (Desiderius Helmschmid, 1544) in Real Armería. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equestrian_Portrait_of_Charles_V , https://www.museodelprado.es/en/whats-on/exhibition/the-art-of-power-the-royal-armoury-and-court/41c17072-f29b-41b5-b0a1-2a7d45d4f8e4
- Alcázar fire 24 Dec 1734, painting damaged and restored. Titian made count palatine 1533. Charles abdicated 1556, Yuste.
- Titian's 1554 letter to Philip on viewing Danaë and Venus and Adonis from front/back. https://www.museodelprado.es/en/whats-on/exhibition/titian-danae-venus-and-adonis-the-early-poesie/821a6867-321a-4570-a19a-db4da7e2042c
- Rubens married Helena Fourment Dec 1630 (she 16, he 53); Prado sees her in the left Grace; kept by Rubens, bought by Philip IV from his estate; ordered burned, saved in Real Academia de Bellas Artes (Prado). DailyArt names Charles III, 1788; script says "accounts differ on which king". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Three_Graces_(Rubens,_Madrid)
- Rubens in Madrid 1628-29, Anglo-Spanish peace mission, friendship with Velázquez and Escorial visit per Pacheco; knighted by Charles I.
