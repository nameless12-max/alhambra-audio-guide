## Key facts and sources
- Family of Charles IV: painted Aranjuez/Madrid spring-summer 1800 after Goya named First Court Painter (1799). Figure identification, faceless woman (possibly Maria Antonia of Naples, Ferdinand's future bride, speculative), Goya at left in shadow, Las Meninas model, Robert Hughes "nonsense"/"act of flattery": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_IV_of_Spain_and_His_Family
- "Corner grocer who has just won the lottery": attributed to Théophile Gautier on Wikipedia with no footnote. Original text not traced. Script puts it in the A voice as "usually credited". Sometimes misattributed to Renoir.
- Godoy/queen affair: contemporary rumour, unproven (A voice).
- Naked Maja in Godoy's collection by 1800 (seen in his cabinet Nov 1800, often cited); Rokeby Venus also Godoy's. Inquisition order 16 March 1815 for Goya to appear and explain the majas: https://fundaciongoyaenaragon.es/eng/obra/la-maja-desnuda/582 . Outcome undocumented. To Prado 1901 from the Academy of San Fernando. Model unknown; Alba = legend, Pepita Tudó = conjecture: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_maja_desnuda
- Sliding-panel display of the two Majas: popular story, undocumented (A voice).
- 1930 stamps (three values, Goya anniversary issue, printed by Waterlow): US ban documented; no returned cover known to philatelists: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naked_Maja_(postage_stamps)
- 1814 commission; Mamelukes; 1938 lorry accident at Benicarló damaging Second of May; 2007-2008 restoration: Prado page above and https://www.museodelprado.es/en/learn/research/studies-and-restorations/resource/the-restoration-of-the-2nd-and-3rd-of-may/19cdd154-67be-4dbd-a5cd-a071d426e54c . Sources differ on destination (Girona per Prado vs Geneva); script just says "north along the coast". One Fundación Goya text dates the crash 1936 (minority).
- Third of May: lantern, white shirt, mark on right hand, friar, Kenneth Clark "first great picture which can be called revolutionary", Manet Maximilian, Picasso Guernica / Massacre in Korea: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Third_of_May_1808 . Script paraphrases Goya's 1814 petition, not quoted verbatim.
- Goya-watched-executions story: late servant's account, treated as legend (A voice). From memory, not re-sourced today.
- Black Paintings: house bought 1819; painted c. 1819/1820-1823; untitled, titles from Brugada inventory; d'Erlanger bought 1873, transfer by Salvador Martínez Cubells from 1874, paint loss; shown/hoped to sell Paris 1878; donated 1881; Saturn faced Leocadia in ground-floor room; Junquera 2003 attribution challenge rejected by Glendinning and Prado: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Paintings , Prado Saturn page above.
- Biography from general knowledge (standard, not re-checked today): born Fuendetodos 1746; illness 1792-93 leaves him deaf; grave illness late 1819; left for France 1824 on pretext of taking waters; Bordeaux; died 16 April 1828 aged 82; house demolished early 1900s.
