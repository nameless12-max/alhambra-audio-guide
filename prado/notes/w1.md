## Welcome
- Villanueva, 1785, Charles III, Natural History Cabinet; Charles died 1788; French troops used it as cavalry barracks and gunpowder store in the Peninsular War. https://www.museodelprado.es/en/museum/history-of-the-museum ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_del_Prado
- Opened 19 Nov 1819. María Isabel de Braganza credited as promoter (Wikipedia: Ferdinand VII "encouraged by his wife"). Her death Dec 1818 is from my own knowledge, not re-checked this session. The 311-works figure was found in one weak source only; not used.
- Cavia hoax: El Liberal, 25 Nov 1891, "La catástrofe de anoche"; explanation next day "Por qué he incendiado el Museo de Pinturas"; two real small fires July 1891. https://periergeia.org/en/the-fire-that-devastated-the-prado-museum ; https://www.elindependiente.com/tendencias/cultura/2021/08/30/el-dia-que-el-museo-del-prado-ardio/amp/
- Evacuation: Valencia Nov 1936 to Feb 1938, Torres de Serranos; Geneva 1939, League of Nations; Geneva exhibition summer 1939; left Geneva 5 Sept 1939. https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2016/06/01/how-the-spanish-republic-saved-the-prados-masterpieces . Counts disagree (353 vs 391 paintings); no figure used.
- Picasso named director 1936, never took up post in person: well known, but in this session found only in a weak source (dailyartmagazine). Worth a second check.

## Annunciation
- c. 1425-26, for San Domenico, Fiesole; left 1611. Sale to fund a bell tower and purchase for Duke of Lerma, given to Dominicans in Valladolid: fascinatingspain.com. Prado acquisitions text says Mario Farnese gave it to Lerma: script flags the disagreement. Descalzas Reales, to Prado 1861 through Federico de Madrazo: fascinatingspain.com; artribune.com 2019.
- Light ray from top left with dove; Adam and Eve expelled; five predella scenes: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annunciation_(Fra_Angelico,_Madrid)
- 2019 restoration for bicentenary exhibition: friendsofflorence.org 2019 press PDF.
- Not used: swallow detail, "hands of God" in the corner (unverified), dimensions (Wikipedia figure looks wrong).

## Mona Lisa
- 1666 Alcázar inventory; in Prado since 1819; walnut panel; infrared shows corrections matching Leonardo's; black repaint not before c. 1750; restored 2011-12; to Louvre "Sainte Anne" show 29 Mar to 25 Jun 2012; Melzi or Salaì, others propose Yáñez or Llanos. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prado_Mona_Lisa ; theartnewspaper earliest-copy article; 2012 press (left for Paris 13 Mar).
- Leonardo's note calling Salaì thief, liar, obstinate, glutton: well known, from memory, not re-checked.
- Louvre original never fully cleaned, yellow varnish: general knowledge, softened in script.

## Descent from the Cross
- Leuven Great Crossbowmen guild, chapel Our Lady Outside the Walls, c. 1435 (some say before 1443); crossbows in spandrels; Christ and Mary's matching curves; skull of Adam; Panofsky on tears; c. 1548 exchanged for Coxcie copy and an organ; Mary of Hungary (Mary of Austria), Binche; Philip II inherited 1558; El Pardo chapel before 1564; Escorial inventory 1574; Valencia 1936, Geneva 1939, Prado Sept 1939; 1990s restoration with George Bisacca of the Met. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Descent_from_the_Cross_(van_der_Weyden) ; https://canon.codart.nl/artwork/descent-from-the-cross/
- Size: 204.5 x 261.5 cm (Codart) vs 220 x 262 cm (Wikipedia). Script says "more than two metres high".
- Shipwreck: attributed to Karel van Mander in one 2026 lecture text (campobaeza.com); otherwise weak sources. Kept in A voice as legend.
- Rogier named city painter of Brussels 1436: from memory; script says "about to be named".
