## Key facts used
- Las Meninas 1656; 318 x 276 cm; Alcázar; title "Las Meninas" first in 1843 Prado catalogue; earlier inventories "the family". Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Meninas ; Smarthistory: https://smarthistory.org/diego-velazquez-las-meninas
- Sitters: Margarita (b. 1651), María Agustina Sarmiento, Isabel de Velasco, Mari Bárbola, Nicolasito Pertusato, Marcela de Ulloa, unnamed guardadamas, José Nieto Velázquez (queen's aposentador), Philip IV and Mariana in mirror. Source: Wikipedia, Prado.
- Santiago: knighted 1659 after inquiry with 148 witnesses (script says "more than a hundred") and papal dispensation (Alexander VII); died 1660. Cross added after 1659; king-painted-it story is legend (A voice).
- Margarita married Leopold I 1666 aged 15, died 1673 aged 21; one child survived infancy.
- 1734 Christmas Eve Alcázar fire; Meninas saved, damaged.
- Giordano "theology of painting", reported by Palomino (1724).
- Picasso, 1957, Cannes (La Californie), 58 works, given to Museu Picasso Barcelona 1968; dog becomes his dachshund Lump.
- Breda: siege Aug 1624 to 5 June 1625; Justin of Nassau, Spinola; honourable terms; painted c. 1634-35 for the Hall of Realms, Buen Retiro; Velázquez sailed with Spinola from Barcelona, 1629; Spinola died 1630. Hall of Realms becoming Prado extension. Self-portrait at far right: tradition, unconfirmed (A voice).
- Dwarf renaming: Prado renamed in 2018; the seated dwarf long called Sebastián de Morra is now "El Primo". Source: Anales del IIE 2023 (https://scielo.org.mx/pdf/aiie/v45n123/0185-1276-aiie-45-123-119.pdf); Christie's lot note dissents in part. Script says "Not every scholar agrees".
- Manet to Fantin-Latour, 1865, on Pablo de Valladolid: paraphrased, not quoted.
- Spinners c. 1655-60, Arachne myth (Ovid, Metamorphoses VI). Script simplifies Ovid's ending to "turns Arachne into a spider".

## Story rewrite (STORY_BRIEF.md)
- Value line, p09: no documented price or insurance figure for Las Meninas found; script says it has no price and will never be sold. Comparison used: Velázquez's Portrait of Juan de Pareja sold at Christie's London, 1970, for 2.31 million pounds to the Metropolitan Museum, then the highest auction price for any painting (https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437869 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portrait_of_Juan_de_Pareja).
- Civil War evacuation: Prado masterpieces incl. Las Meninas taken via Valencia and Catalonia to Geneva, 1939, returned the same year (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Meninas).
- Legend (A voice): French writer "where is the painting?", usually attributed to Théophile Gautier; source not pinned down.
- Spinola "died feeling his king had not valued him": widely reported (he is said to have complained his honour was taken, Castelnuovo Scrivia, 1630); hedged as "it was said".
- Manet's reaction to Pablo de Valladolid: letter to Fantin-Latour, 1865, paraphrased.
- Knighthood 28 Nov 1659, death 6 Aug 1660: "less than a year later".
