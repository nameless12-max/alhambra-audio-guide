# stop: 0

## The painter in the shadows

N: Think back to the first room of this story. A deaf man stands in the dark at the back of a royal portrait, watching a family glitter.

N: He went on watching. He watched the most powerful man in Spain unlock a secret room for a naked woman who stared right back. He watched the Inquisition come knocking. He watched his city rise with knives against an empire, and he watched it shot down by lantern light.

## The house with the black walls

...

N: And when it was all over, he went home, shut the door, and painted for no one. A god eating his child. Two men sinking as they fight. A small dog, looking up at nothing. Then exile, and a quiet death far from home.

N: He began his life painting picnics and dances for the king's tapestries. He ended it painting the darkest pictures of his age. Between the two, he taught painting how to look at power, and at war, and not look away.

## Where the story goes next

...

N: The story does not end here. A short walk from this building, at the Reina Sofía museum, hangs Picasso's Guernica. It is a scream about a town bombed from the air, and Picasso painted it with the man in the white shirt in mind. Go and stand in front of it, tomorrow or on your next visit, and look for Goya's open arms.

N: Thank you for walking with me through the Prado, Monica. Goodbye for now.
