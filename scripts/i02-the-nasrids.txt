# title: The Nasrids in two minutes
# stop: 0

## The founding

N: The Nasrids in two minutes.

N: Everything in the palaces was built by one family, the Nasrids. They ruled the last Muslim kingdom in the Iberian peninsula.

N: In the twelve thirties and twelve forties, the Christian kingdoms of Castile and Aragon were swallowing the great Muslim cities. Córdoba, Valencia and Seville all fell. In those same years, one man carved out a kingdom in the mountains around Granada. His name was Muhammad ibn Nasr, and he ruled as Muhammad the First.

N: In twelve thirty-eight he started building on this hill.

## A small rich state

...

N: His kingdom lasted two hundred and fifty years. It survived by being useful and careful. Its sultans paid tribute to Castile as vassals. Sometimes they fought beside it. When they needed help, they leaned on Morocco.

N: Granada filled up with Muslims leaving the conquered cities. It grew rich on silk, on trade and on farming.

N: So the palaces you are about to see are the luxury of a small state. It was wealthy, and it was always under threat.

## Rulers and the fall

...

N: Much of the finest work belongs to just two rulers. The first is Yusuf the First, who ruled until thirteen fifty-four. The second is his son, Muhammad the Fifth.

N: Muhammad the Fifth ruled twice. He lost the throne in thirteen fifty-nine, won it back three years later, and then ruled until thirteen ninety-one. You will hear more of that story at stop two.

...

N: The end came in fourteen ninety-two. It followed ten years of war, made worse by civil war inside the ruling family itself.

N: Keep three names in your ear. Muhammad the First founded the kingdom. Yusuf the First and his son, Muhammad the Fifth, built most of what you will see.
