# title: Welcome
# stop: 0

## Where you are

N: Welcome to the Alhambra. This walk starts where your ticket says you must be on time, in the Nasrid Palaces.

N: From there it moves outward. First come the council hall, the throne tower and the private Palace of the Lions. Then the older Partal, and the tower-houses on the walls. Then across to the Generalife, the summer estate. Last, back along the old town street to the emperor's palace and the fortress where it all began.

N: There is one track for each stop. Each one tells you where to stand, what to look at, and why it matters. Pause whenever you like. The building will wait.

## The second voice

...

A: I am the second voice. You will hear me when a story is legend, when experts disagree, or when a detail is widely told but we could not trace it to a solid source.

## Etiquette and timing

...

N: Inside the palace rooms, please keep your voice down.

N: The route through the palaces runs one way, and the order of the rooms can change. If yours differs, follow the signs, and play the track for the room you are in.

N: Allow four to five hours. This walk lingers. When you are ready, make your way to the entrance of the Nasrid Palaces. Just past the ticket check is the Mexuar. That is stop one.
