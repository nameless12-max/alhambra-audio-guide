# title: Stop 11. San Miguel el Alto: the Templars' baptism of blood
# stop: 11
# room: Church of San Miguel el Alto and Calle de la Soledad, on the high ground just south-east of the Alcázar

## The knights on the hill

N: Stop eleven. The church of San Miguel el Alto, Saint Michael on High, stands on the highest ground of the old town, a few minutes' walk south of the Alcázar. It is shut at night. Look up for its tall square tower.

N: Just beside it, on Calle de la Soledad, is a long, plain house called the Casa del Temple. The House of the Temple. Toledans have always said the Knights Templar lived here.

...

N: The Templars were warrior monks who grew rich, until their order was dissolved and its leaders burned. Wherever they had lived, people whispered about treasure.

A: In Toledo the whispers reached the archbishop himself. They say that, some sixty years later, Archbishop Tenorio sent men down into the tunnels under this church and the Templar houses, to find the gold. They found no gold. They found a cross. And they found the bodies of knights, dried and whole, laid out in the dark.

N: Where did the dead knights come from? Here is the story.

## The night before the battle

A: A Christian army has been crushed in the south. The Moors are marching on Toledo. The Templars are given this stretch of the wall. The night before the assault, their commander gathers them in this church to pray.

A: He looks at their faces. He cannot bear not knowing. He asks God for a sign. Show me who will die tomorrow.

A: And on the red crosses of some of the knights' cloaks, the figure of Christ appears.

...

A: The commander makes his choice. Better living warriors than dead saints. At dawn he sends only the unmarked knights to the wall. The marked ones stay behind, praying.

A: The attack is beaten back. Not one knight falls on the wall. They return to the church and find their brothers lying on the stones, dead, already dried like mummies. The water in the font has turned to blood.

A: Only when the commander dips his cross into the font does the water run clear again. God had chosen who would die. The commander had only changed where.

## What is true

N: That is legend, passed down by word of mouth. The archbishop's search and the mummies are tradition too. But Toledo's dry soil did preserve bodies in its crypts. And a Templar emblem really is carved on a capital inside this church.

N: The Casa del Temple, when it was restored, turned out to be an Islamic house, older than the knights.

N: Opposite the church door is the House of the Goblin, the Casa del Duende. Its cellar, they say, runs under the church and the houses around it.

...

N: Stand by the tower and think of the commander, alone with his sign. Would you want to know?

## Walk on

N: Next, just down the hill, a Christ on a wall and the marks of a hundred swords. That is stop twelve.
