## Not verified

- l01: the white paving stone marking where the horse knelt; Puerta del Sol as nearest gate is from memory.
- l02: painted turbaned riders and the inscription wording are the standard version from memory, not checked in a primary text; "within a year".
- l03: Table of Solomon details (two generals quarrelling, broken leg, divine name); medieval rumour of necromancy taught in caves.
- l04: exact location of the well (south of old town, small square, plaque wording); "open to the street".
- l05: location wording (downhill from Puerta del Cambrón); the lowered right arm as popular version; that today's figure is a replacement.
- l06: Bécquer sketching churches with Valeriano; that French troops did damage in Toledo's convents (general, not checked).
- l07: water carried up by donkey; that Juanelo was not paid in full; the archbishop's palace as destination (one variant); Callejón del Diablo from a single travel blog.
- l08: Calle de las Bulas location from a travel blog; the feast being at the Alcázar on the campaign eve per one summary.
- Opening hours not checked tonight for any site; all assumed closed after dark except streets and the well.
