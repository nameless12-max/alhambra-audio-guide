## Location settled: Christ of the Skull

Bécquer's text has the knights leave the Alcázar feast, cross Zocodover and reach "la calle del Cristo", then walk to the cathedral square. That fits the present Calle del Cristo de la Calavera (OSM way 1098699681), just south of Zocodover and the Alcázar. Calle de las Bulas (south-west, by the Jewish quarter) comes from one travel blog only and is not in the text. Pin kept on the named street. l08's opening still says "Toledans place it near Calle de las Bulas"; that line was not changed (no rewrite of l08 allowed).

## Open points

- l08 says "Stop eight, the last one", and l00 does not state a count, so neither was edited. New tracks are stops nine to eighteen; l18 ends by sending her back to stop eight for the walk home.
- l07 says the Callejón del Diablo is "near Zocodover"; OSM puts it about 250 m south of the square, by Calle del Cristo de la Calavera.
