## Building facts (museum history page)
https://museoreinasofia.es/en/museo/history
Charles III hospital; Hermosilla and Sabatini; work stopped 1788 with a third built; closed 1965; demolition rumours; monument by royal decree 1977; restoration from 1980 (Fernández Alba); art centre April 1986; museum by decree 1988; permanent collection opened 10 Sep 1992; lift towers with Ian Ritchie (late 1980s); Nouvel extension begun 2001, opened Sep 2005, +60% surface.

## Story facts
- Woman in Blue: 1901 Madrid, Arte Joven period, sent to 1901 General Exhibition of Fine Arts, "went unnoticed", Picasso left for Paris solo show, forgotten for decades, later to Museo Nacional de Arte Moderno, Reina Sofía 1988 (museum page). "Rejected prize / never collected it" not confirmed: legend voice. Arte Joven co-founder (Francisco de Asís Soler) described only as "a Catalan friend".
- Un mundo: 1929 Valladolid, 290 x 310 cm, Autumn Salon 1929, Gómez de la Serna "seventeen-year-old girl", Franz Roh book (museum). Born 1911 Portbou, moved Valladolid 1927, lessons with Cellino Perotti, married Emilio Grau Sala, died 2013 aged 101: from general sources/AWARE https://awarewomenartists.com/en/artiste/angeles-santos-torroella/ . Running away / sanatorium: Artículo 14 only, legend voice.
- Figure at the Window: 1925, Ana María, Cadaqués, "perhaps the most important" of the Ana María portraits, at least a dozen 1923-26 (museum). Book 1949 "Salvador Dalí visto por su hermana"; break over Gala; nearly 40 years estranged (secondary). Lorca at Cadaqués 1925: standard biography, not checked this session.
- La verbena: 1927, shown 1928 in show organised by Ortega y Gasset, imagery per museum text (Paloma Esteban Leal). Hatless Puerta del Sol walk: Mallo's own later account, legend voice. Exile 1937 Buenos Aires, return 1960s, died 1995: standard biography, not rechecked. Retrospective at Reina Sofía 8 Oct 2025 - 16 Mar 2026 (closed now).
- Great Masturbator: late summer 1929, Gala stayed in Cadaqués while Éluard returned to Paris, self-portrait reading (Santos Torroella), grasshopper fear (Ades), acquired 1990 via Dalí bequest (museum). Lorca and the Chien Andalou title: Buñuel memoir claim, legend voice.
